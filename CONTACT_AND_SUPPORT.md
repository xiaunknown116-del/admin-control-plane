## Escalation Procedure

1. **Initial Contact:** Email support@apexcapitalweb.com with detailed description
2. **Acknowledgment:** You'll receive a ticket number and expected resolution timeline
3. **Investigation:** Our team will review and escalate if necessary
4. **Resolution:** You'll be notified of the outcome and any follow-up actions required
5. **Dual-Control Approval:** For sensitive issues, resolution requires documented approval from multiple authorized personnel
